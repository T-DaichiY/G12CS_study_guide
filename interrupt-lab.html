When an interrupt comes, where does the unfinished work go?

A program is running. Suddenly, an interrupt arrives. The CPU puts the unfinished work on the kernel stack, uses the IDT to choose the right ISR, and then takes the work back. Choose different interrupts and see which IDT row is used.

Six interrupts and the IDT

The six steps are the same for every interrupt. What changes is where the signal comes from and which ISR the IDT chooses. Press "Show" to jump to STEP 4 for that interrupt.

Structure

The six steps are made of two pairs

Do not just memorise the order. Learn which steps go together. The word "restore" appears twice, so be clear about what is being restored each time.

Interrupt switch: OFF and ON

STEP 1 disable⇄STEP 6 re-enable

In STEP 6, the thing restored is the ability to accept interrupts. It is not the program's work.

Unfinished work: save and restore

STEP 2 push⇄STEP 5 pop

STEP 5 restores the registers and the program continues. Both happen in STEP 5.

Analogy

Your phone rings while you are reading

STEP 1The phone rings. You turn on "Do Not Disturb".Interrupt switch OFF
STEP 2You put a bookmark in your book.Save the work on the kernel stack
STEP 3You check who is calling and if it is urgent.Identify the source and priority
STEP 4You check your contacts list and answer the call.Use the IDT to find and run the ISR
STEP 5You open the book at the bookmark and keep reading.Restore the work and continue
STEP 6You turn off "Do Not Disturb".Interrupt switch ON
Exam tips

Common mistakes

✕ Common answer

The CPU switches to the interrupt immediately, even in the middle of an instruction.

○ Correct

The CPU checks the interrupt register at the end of each FDE cycle, then switches.

✕ Common answer

In STEP 6, the CPU checks if there are more interrupts.

○ Correct

In STEP 6, the interrupts disabled in STEP 1 are enabled again.

✕ Common answer

In STEP 6, the saved process continues.

○ Correct

The process continues in STEP 5. Restoring and continuing happen together.

✕ Common answer

The ISR runs (no mention of the IDT).

○ Correct

The IDT is used to find the ISR's address, which is loaded into the PC.

✕ Common answer

There are two sources: hardware and software.

○ Correct

There are three: hardware, exception, and trap (software interrupt).

Analogy

STEP 3你看看是谁打来的，急不急。识别来源和优先级
STEP 4你查看通讯录，然后接电话。用 IDT 找到并运行 ISR
STEP 5你翻到书签那一页，继续读。恢复工作并继续
STEP 6你关闭“勿扰模式”。中断开关 ON
Exam tips

常见错误

✕ 常见答案

CPU 会立刻切换到中断，即使指令执行到一半。

○ 正确

CPU 在每个 FDE 周期结束时检查中断寄存器，然后才切换。

✕ 常见答案

在 STEP 6，CPU 检查是否还有其他中断。

○ 正确

在 STEP 6，重新启用在 STEP 1 中被禁用的中断。

✕ 常见答案

在 STEP 6，被保存的进程继续运行。

○ 正确

进程在 STEP 5 继续运行。恢复和继续是一起发生的。

✕ 常见答案

运行 ISR（没有提到 IDT）。

○ 正确

用 IDT 找到 ISR 的地址，并把它放进 PC。

✕ 常见答案

来源只有两种：硬件和软件。

○ 正确

有三种：hardware、exception 和 trap（软件中断）。

อินเทอร์รัปต์ 6 แบบกับ IDT
